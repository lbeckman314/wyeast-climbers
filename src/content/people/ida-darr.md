Ida Darr climbed with the Wy'east Climbers and, with her husband, founded Portland's Mountain Shop in 1937. In July 1940 she was one of the party that carried the body of Ranger, Mount Hood's famous climbing dog, up the mountain for burial just below the summit.

*Research lead:* Conrad's book is the place to look for her climbing record. A correction we've received describes her as a stronger climber and organizer than her husband; we'd like a source for that. Women climbers of the 1930s are badly under-documented, which makes her a strong subject for a future exhibit.
