Barney Macnab captained the Nile River Yacht Club, a Portland *ski* club that, despite its name, had nothing to do with the Nile or with boats. Conrad describes a "friendly rivalry" between it and the Wy'easters.

The ski patrol's history credits Macnab and Wy'east president Everett Darr with persuading the Forest Service to organize care for injured skiers (a point we're checking; see Hank Lewis). When the Mount Hood Ski Patrol incorporated on March 2, 1938, Macnab (national number 17) became its first president.
