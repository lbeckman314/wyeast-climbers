Long before there was any formal patrol, Wy'easters were helping injured climbers down the mountain. [According to Conrad](https://onmounthood.com/2015/02/23/a-mount-hood-qa-ric-conrad-author-of-mount-hood-adventures-of-the-wyeast-climbers-1930-1942/), they had "assisted injured alpinists in the early to mid-1930s."
