[A summit register entry](https://mounthoodhistory.com/mount-hood/ranger-and-laddie-on-mount-hood-legendary-climbing-dogs/) records "five Wy'East Climbers from Portland" reaching the top at 6:15 a.m. with three dogs: Ranger, his brother Laddie, and a dog named Wolf.
