The club built a cabin on Mount Hood in 1934, in a cluster of club cabins that included the Nile River Yacht Club's and the Forest Service's older Phlox Point Cabin. [A Mount Hood Museum guide](https://mthoodmuseum.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/A-Guide-to-SKISTERICAL-MAP-of-MT.pdf) calls it "the latest Wy'east Climbers cabin," which suggests the club had others before it. The cabins were removed in the 1960s because they lacked septic systems and fell short of building codes.
