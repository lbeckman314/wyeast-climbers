A group of young Portland men, several of them already Mazamas or members of the Trails Club of Oregon, wanted a smaller and more committed circle of climbing partners. They were drawn to unclimbed terrain on Mount Hood.

[Conrad places the club's beginnings in 1930](https://onmounthood.com/2015/02/23/a-mount-hood-qa-ric-conrad-author-of-mount-hood-adventures-of-the-wyeast-climbers-1930-1942/), and [the Oregon Historical Society's listing for his book](https://www.ohs.org/shop/museum-store/books-and-publications/mount-hood-adventures-of-the-wy-east-climbers.cfm) gives December 1930. [A PNWSAR history](https://pnwsar.org/history) gives 1931 instead, and adds a memorable detail: the club organized after a hike up the Larch Mountain Trail from Multnomah Falls, holding its first meeting "in the men's restroom of the Multnomah Falls Lodge."
