A group of young Portland men, several of them already Mazamas or members of the Trails Club of Oregon, wanted a smaller and more committed circle of climbing partners. They were drawn to unclimbed terrain on Mount Hood.

**Sources disagree on the year.** [Conrad's book](https://www.ohs.org/shop/museum-store/books-and-publications/mount-hood-adventures-of-the-wy-east-climbers.cfm) and [Jack Grauer's history](http://www.alpenglow.org/ski-history/notes/book/grauer-1975.html) both give December 1930. [A PNWSAR history](https://pnwsar.org/history) gives 1931 and adds a memorable detail: the club organized after a hike up the Larch Mountain Trail from Multnomah Falls, holding its first meeting "in the men's restroom of the Multnomah Falls Lodge."
