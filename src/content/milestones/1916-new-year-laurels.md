The "New Year Laurels" go to the first alpinist to stand on Mount Hood's summit in the new year, and climbers have been chasing them [since at least 1916](https://onmounthood.com/2015/02/23/a-mount-hood-qa-ric-conrad-author-of-mount-hood-adventures-of-the-wyeast-climbers-1930-1942/). The Wy'east Climbers, along with a few unaffiliated "outlaws," helped make this midwinter race popular.
