The Darrs opened a climbing and ski shop on NE Broadway in Portland, and later kept a Mountain Shop in Government Camp. [The Mountain Shop is still in business](https://mountainshop.net/about-us/our-story.html) and calls itself the oldest shop of its kind in the country that it knows of.

*To verify:* when the Darrs left the Portland shop and opened in Government Camp. A correction we've received puts it around 1948, when they sold the Portland shop.
