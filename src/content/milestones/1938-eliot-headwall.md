[Conrad credits](https://onmounthood.com/2015/02/23/a-mount-hood-qa-ric-conrad-author-of-mount-hood-adventures-of-the-wyeast-climbers-1930-1942/) the Wy'east Climbers with the first ascent of the steep, crumbling Eliot Glacier Headwall on Hood's north side. The party is given as Russ McJury and Joe Leuthold.

**Sources disagree on the year.** Two sources give 1938, and [Mountain Project gives 1935](https://www.mountainproject.com/route/108214095/eliot-glacier-headwall).
