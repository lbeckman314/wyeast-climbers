Ranger died on July 1, 1940. A group of Wy'east Climbers wrapped his body in cloth, strapped it to a board, and carried it up the mountain. The party named in [the account](https://mounthoodhistory.com/mount-hood/ranger-and-laddie-on-mount-hood-legendary-climbing-dogs/) is Ole Lien, Jim Harlow, Ralph Calkin, Ida Darr, Ed Meyer and Paul Parker. Just below the summit they dug a grave with their ice axes and built a stone cairn over it. They recorded the burial in the summit register on July 7, 1940.
