Franklin D. Roosevelt dedicated the WPA-built [Timberline Lodge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timberline_Lodge) on Hood's south slope. The lodge brought a wave of skiers and tourists to the mountain, and with them more injuries. That gave the Wy'easters' push for an organized patrol new urgency.
